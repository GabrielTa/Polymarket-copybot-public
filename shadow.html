📖 In plain terms: this page tracks the bets the bot chose NOT to make. Whenever a signal is strong enough to bet but a safety filter blocks it (price too cheap, resolves too far out, or a banned bet type like spreads / exact-score), we log it here as a pretend $100 bet and follow it to see how it would have turned out. No real or paper money is used.

Why it matters: if the bets we blocked keep losing, our filters are doing their job ✅. If they keep winning, we're being too strict and should loosen up ⚠️. Each row only shows a win/loss once its real market settles, so the tables fill in over days.
