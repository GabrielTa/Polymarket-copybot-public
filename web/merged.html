📖 In plain terms: an experiment that tried to combine our "conviction" edge with a friend's Kelly sizing math. Kelly is a formula that stakes more when your edge is bigger — but it only bets when your win rate clearly beats the market price. The result: it didn't work. That rule rules out our best bets (confident favorites at higher prices) and instead forces cheap bets that lose. We keep this page as an honest record of what we tested and why we set it aside. No real bets.

Takeaway: our edge is "expensive conviction" (many sharp leaders on a favorite), not "cheap value" — so the Kelly formula fights it instead of helping. The winning idea lives on the Hybrid page.
