Category ? The market category (Sports, Crypto, Politics…). We block Politics and unknown categories from entry. Market ? The prediction market we have an open bet on. Click the link to view it live on Polymarket. My Bet ? Which outcome we are holding and the direction.

Green = we bet this outcome WINS (BUY).
Red = we bet this outcome LOSES (SELL/NO). 		Entry ? The price we paid per share when we opened this position. Polymarket prices run 0–1, where 1.00 = certainty. E.g. 0.62 means the market implied a 62% chance at entry. Current ? The live mid-price for this outcome right now. Refreshes every 60 seconds. Higher than entry = market has moved in our favour. Cost ? How much paper money we staked on this position (entry price × shares bought). This is what we risk losing if the outcome is wrong. Pot. Payout ? If this position resolves YES: total cash returned (shares × $1.00) and the implied return % over our cost. E.g. $120 (+33%) means we get $120 back on a $90 stake. Unreal. P&L ? Unrealised profit or loss right now if we were to close at the current price. Green = winning, Red = losing. This fluctuates until the market resolves. P&L % ? Price movement since entry as a percentage. Shows how far the market has moved in our direction regardless of position size. +10% means the probability has risen 10 points since we entered. Resolves ? When this market is scheduled to close and pay out. Orange = resolves within 7 days. Green = resolving imminently. Str ? Signal strength — how many leaders were in agreement when we entered this position. ×4 = 4 leaders agreed. Higher = stronger conviction. Held ? How long this position has been open. Longer holds tend to win more — positions under 1h are still settling. ⚠️ shown if price has dropped close to the adverse-exit threshold (entry × 0.65).
