🕐 Pre-Game vs In-Play

The biggest edge discovery
← Main dashboard   Latency →   Hybrid →
📖 In plain terms: we discovered the bot often bets on games that have already kicked off (in-play), not just before them (pre-game). This matters a lot: pre-game bets get in before the price fully reflects the game; in-play bets pay the live price for a copied opinion that's already stale. A bet is IN-PLAY if we opened it after the scheduled game start. This page compares the two — if pre-game wins clearly, "only bet before kickoff" becomes a top filter.
