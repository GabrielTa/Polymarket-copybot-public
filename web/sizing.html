📖 In plain terms: this page is about how much to bet, not what to bet. It replays the exact same trades the bot already made and asks: "what if we'd staked different amounts?" It compares two ideas against your real book: Flat (same stake every time — what the bot does now) vs Conviction-weighted (bet more when more leaders agree — 1.5× on 5-leader signals, 2× on 6+). A bet's win/loss doesn't depend on its size, so we can test this safely by re-weighting past results. No real bets.

How to read it: the fair scoreboard is ROI (profit per $1 risked), since the two bet different amounts. Higher ROI at similar drawdown = the better way to size.
