🎯 Closing Line Value (CLV)

Skill vs luck — the pro metric
← Main dashboard   Latency →   Shadow →
📖 In plain terms: when we buy at a price, did the market later move toward us before the game (we got a good price = skill) or away from us (we bought the top = exit liquidity)? That's CLV. Unlike win/loss — which is mostly luck at these odds — CLV shows real skill in dozens of bets, not hundreds. We measure it against the price just before the event (never the win/lose resolution). Applied to leaders, it separates who actually moves markets from who just got lucky.
⚠️ Verify before trusting: a wrong "close" price would poison every number here. Hand-check the sample rows at the bottom against the Polymarket price chart before acting on any verdict. Closes that already hit 0/1 are auto-excluded.
